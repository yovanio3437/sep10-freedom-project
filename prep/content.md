##### Hardware
* IMU
  * Tracks the balls acceleration and movement in 3d
  * It uses the movement to register a touch
  * Gives precise data
  * Real time data
  * Use the touches to register offsides with the help of VAR
  * Makes offside calls more faster
  * Helps track players
  * Can help analyze handballs
  * Helps analyze penalties
  * Lightweight inside the ball
  * Does not altar the performance of the ball
  * Used in Major FIFA competitions
  * [IMU](https://meshworld.in/_astro/trionda-ball.Dsrzh6Sc_2m2bOi.webp)
* Referee camera
  * Small and lightweight for the referee to run with
  * HD audio and video
  * Helps give clarity for on field decisions
  * Can be used during VAR review to help give better angles of a foul
  * New angle to see how goals are scored
  * Video records Fouls to show the fans a better angle
  * [Ref camera](https://cdn3.sportngin.com/attachments/photo/0f24-196395643/RefCam_Website_Mind_Map__11_x_8.5_in__large.png)
